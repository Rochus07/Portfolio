Learning Reflection

Throughout this course, I gained valuable knowledge on how HTML, CSS, JavaScript, and Tailwind CSS work together to build responsive, user-friendly websites. I learned that web development goes beyond writing code it's about making websites functional, accessible, and visually appealing.

The biggest challenges I faced were making the layout responsive across screen sizes, debugging JavaScript errors, and keeping the design consistent in terms of colors, fonts, and spacing. Balancing creativity with technical limitations was also difficult at times.

To overcome these, I studied tutorials, tested the website repeatedly, and broke tasks into smaller parts to avoid getting overwhelmed. Exploring different techniques and examples also helped me improve the responsiveness and overall look of the site.

Overall, this course strengthened my technical and creative skills and taught me that building a website requires patience, practice, and attention to detail. Every challenge was a learning opportunity, and this experience has motivated me to keep growing as a developer.
